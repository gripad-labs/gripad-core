# Tokenomics

This note describes how value and responsibility are meant to flow through Gripad. It lists no supply, price or allocation figures. Figures will be published only alongside a stated contract address.

## What a launch token is

Tokens launched through Gripad belong to their creators. The protocol does not mint supply for itself and does not take a hidden share. Each launch sets its own supply and distribution through its presets.

## Protocol fee

The protocol intends to charge a small fee on preset use, paid in SOL by the creator at publish time. The fee funds preset maintenance and audits. The rate will be fixed in the open before any launch uses it, and it will not apply to transfers after publish.

## The protocol's own token

A native token for Gripad is under discussion and is not announced. If one is created it will be governed by the same rule as everything else here: its terms are fixed at creation, written into the token, and published together with its contract address. Until a contract address is stated on the site, there is no official Gripad token.

## Guardrails

- No team allocation that is invisible on-chain.
- No upgrade key that can rewrite a launch's presets after publish without the creator's signature.
- No figure appears in marketing before it appears in a signed, public transaction.

## Treasury

Fees accrue to a multisig whose members and threshold will be listed before the first fee is collected. Spending categories: audits, preset development, infrastructure, and grants for community-built presets.
