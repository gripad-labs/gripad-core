Presets

Shape how an asset behaves.

A preset is a compact program with a single duty. Layer several, tune their limits, then settings harden into pool law.

Wallet ceiling

Each purchase is weighed against the share its receiver would hold afterward. Cross your line and that purchase simply fails to settle.

Checks: buysPer holderSet once

Named doors

A roster of wallets is frozen at publish time. While the opening window lasts, only those wallets may receive supply; afterward the door swings wide.

Checks: receiverTimed

Stepped gate

The ceiling is a ladder rather than a single figure. Each rung unlocks once liquidity reaches a depth that you choose, keeping early rooms small while depth builds.

Checks: buysDepth-linkedStages

Cooldown lane

Once someone sells, a countdown begins. Any second sale that arrives early gets turned away, which slows rapid exits without freezing supply.

Checks: sellsPer holderCountdown

Slice unlock

Balances gained during opening are cut into portions. Each portion turns sellable along separate schedules, so supply trickles outward gradually.

Checks: sellsSchedule

Depth throttle

The program reads liquidity and limits any single sale to a fraction of what sits beneath it. Thin books get small exits, deep books get larger ones.

Checks: sellsPool-aware

Single venue

Transfers touching any market except your chosen one are refused. Liquidity stays within one room, keeping price history tidy.

Checks: routeOne market

Pool roster

Name a handful of approved pools. Supply flows through those and nowhere else, leaving unknown venues without any way to list it.

Checks: routeShort list

Hand to hand

Plain sends between people pass. Anything aimed at an automated market is declined, which suits community coins that should circulate by hand.

Checks: routeDirect only

Quiet windows

Pick recurring hours when supply rests. Within those hours all movement bounces, and outside them everything flows normally.

Checks: all movesRecurring

Slot spacing

Each holder must let several slots pass between trades. Bots firing many orders at once find that only the first succeeds.

Checks: all movesSlot-based

Slow open

All limits begin tight and loosen along a curve you shape. Early hours stay calm by design while supply grows into its freedom.

Checks: all movesCurveEases off

Flow

Four moves from idea to market.

01

Pick

Browse the shelves and choose presets matching the release you want.

02

Tune

Adjust the edges on each: sizes, timers, lists, plus whichever curve they follow.

03

Publish

Sign once using keys you control. Terms get written straight onto the asset.

04

Hold

All movement is inspected in transit. A move violating a term is simply bounced.

Drill

Ninety seconds. Judge each transfer.

Every card displays a fictional transfer next to the rule it meets. Say YES if the gatekeeper allows it to land, NO if it bounces. All figures are fictional; only the rule shapes mirror those above.

Simulated 1:30

The clock runs for ninety seconds. Each right call earns points, and streaks add a bonus. Keys: Y for YES, N for NO.
